Rule-Based AI is a type of artificial intelligence that makes decisions by following predefined rules created by humans, usually in the form of IF–THEN statements. As shown in the image above, the AI-powered chatbot can understand different ways of expressing a request and respond accordingly, while the rule-based chatbot follows specific predefined options. When a user makes an unexpected request, it may fail to understand and redirect them to the available options or a human agent.
